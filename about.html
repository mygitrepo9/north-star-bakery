Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery focused on bringing fresh, handmade baked goods to our community. We believe a local bakery should be a welcoming place where families, commuters, and neighbors can enjoy quality breads, pastries, and cakes.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe great baked goods begin with quality ingredients. We carefully select ingredients for freshness and quality while supporting a thoughtful, community-centered approach to baking. Our goal is to create products that taste homemade and are prepared with care.
